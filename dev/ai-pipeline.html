WHOLE TRIP

The event path, not a pile of sensors.

The feeder’s NPU sees a possible body. The Rust daemon decides whether that is a moving visitor, whether it is at the bowl, and whether a face can be named. Home Assistant mirrors those results; the card draws them.

DON’T MIX THESE UP

  • Feeding = dispenser hardware is running.
  • Eating = a valid body overlaps the bowl for 3 seconds.
  • Bowl fill = camera/NPU food estimate, not a weight scale.
1

“Cat came by”

It saw a body twice in a row and proved it moved at least once. A static shelf-shaped false positive should never become a saved visit.

2

“Pancake” / “Kitty”

A face vector matched labelled examples strongly enough, with agreement from more than one frame. One attractive guess is intentionally not enough.

3

Scene evidence

The full camera frame is matched to the exact vision timestamp, copied into HA, and fetched by the card from HA—not from a public feeder URL.
